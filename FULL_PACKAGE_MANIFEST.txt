Public package contents
- Analysis scripts: 00_install_packages.R through 10_validate_manuscript_outputs.R, together with ONSEN_config.R and ONSEN_functions.R.
- Final supplementary workbooks: Table_S1.xlsx through Table_S13.xlsx.
- Data-equivalent worksheet mirrors: supplementary_table_source/.
- Compact figure/source data: source_data/.
- Motif metadata/PFMs used for the representative Fig. 7D logos: motifs/.
- RNA-seq sample metadata and public-input provenance: RNAseq_sample_metadata_template.csv and INPUT_PROVENANCE.tsv.
- Public documentation: README.md, DATA_AVAILABILITY.md, REPRODUCIBILITY_NOTES.md, REPRODUCIBILITY_MATRIX.tsv, CITATION.cff, LICENSE and R_PACKAGE_REQUIREMENTS.txt.

Large sequencing/reference inputs are not duplicated in this repository. Their expected filenames, public accessions and analysis roles are documented in INPUT_PROVENANCE.tsv.

The final supplementary set comprises Fig. S1-Fig. S4 and Table S1-Table S13.
